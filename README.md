# OrbitXOS – Orbital Dynamics–Based Space Debris Monitoring and Satellite Forecasting System 

![OrbitXOS Dashboard](/images/IMG_0463.PNG)  

## Overview  
**OrbitXOS** is a web-based **Space Situational Awareness (SSA) platform** designed to **track, visualize, and forecast interactions** between space debris and active satellites across **Low Earth Orbit (LEO), Medium Earth Orbit (MEO), and Geostationary Orbit (GEO)**.  

With the exponential rise in satellite launches and orbital congestion, OrbitXOS addresses the critical challenge of **collision prediction and monitoring**, offering an **integrated solution** for researchers, space organizations, and policymakers.  

---

## Key Features  

- **Satellite & Debris Tracking**  
  Real-time monitoring of thousands of satellites and debris fragments in multiple orbital regions.  
  ![Objects Tracked](/images/IMG_0464.PNG)  

- **3D Earth Visualization**  
  Interactive 3D Earth model displaying orbital paths, debris distribution, and satellite positioning for intuitive exploration of space traffic.  
  ![3D Earth Visualization](/images/IMG_0466.PNG)  

- **Collision Forecasting Engine**  
  AI-powered system predicts potential **satellite–debris collision events**, showing:  
  - Satellite & debris identifiers  
  - Probability of collision (%)  
  - Critical thresholds with confidence levels  
  - Recommended avoidance maneuvers  
  ![Collision Prediction Center](/images/IMG_0465.PNG)  

- **Prediction Dashboard**  
  Dedicated forecasting section highlighting **high-risk collision events** requiring immediate attention.  

- **🛡 Decision Support for Space Safety**  
  Assists **space agencies, satellite operators, and researchers** in making informed decisions on **collision avoidance** and **orbital sustainability**.  

---

## Impact  
OrbitXOS combines **tracking, prediction, and visualization** into a single platform, enhancing **Space Situational Awareness (SSA)**.  

- Prevents **costly satellite collisions**  
- Improves **orbital safety**  
- Contributes to **long-term space sustainability**
